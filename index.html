Intro to Basketball

What is Basketball?

Basketball is a team sport where theres 2 teams and each team has 5 players, there are 5 Positions in basketball, each teams goal is simple, score on the other end of the court and stop the other team from scoring, Basketball can be played in many different ways aswell.

Scoring

2 points for normal shots
3 points from beyond the arc
1 point for free throws

Positions

Point Guard
Shooting Guard
Small Forward
Power Forward
Center

Basic Rules

No running without dribbling
No pushing other players
Game has 4 quarters
